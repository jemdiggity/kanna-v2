# Mobile task composer auto-growth

## Goal

Make the mobile task reply composer grow upward from its baseline through five lines of text, then scroll internally, while shrinking on deletion and resetting after send.

## Scope and constraints

- Preserve the existing attachment row and attach/send controls, keeping those controls anchored as the input grows.
- Preserve keyboard avoidance for the whole composer at every height.
- Add focused headless coverage for the height bounds and component behavior.
- This is a JS-only UI change. Do not change `apps/mobile/src/mobileEnvironments.json` or its `2.2.2` runtime versions.
- Do not change the separate new-task composer or quick-reply editor.
- Reviewer directive (2026-08-23, revision round 1): durably record real iOS
  Simulator captures for 1, 3, 5, and 8+ lines, attachment layout, shrink, and
  post-send reset; rerun the focused composer tests and `./kd test all`.

## Done when

The task reply field renders correctly at 1, 3, 5, and 8+ lines (with internal scrolling beyond five), resets after send, passes mobile type/tests, and is visually verified in the iOS Simulator with the keyboard shown.

## Visual verification (2026-08-23)

Verified in the real Kanna dev client on an iPhone 17 Pro simulator running
iOS 26.5. The current CLI makes `./kd mobile run` physical-device-only, so the
simulator-equivalent canonical flow was `./kd dev up --mobile` (never bare
Expo). Each image in `04026ef3-screenshots/` was captured with
`xcrun simctl io C48044E2-D11B-4A50-993D-D571CA8462E7 screenshot`.

- `01`/`02`/`03` show 1, 3, and 5 lines at 40, 80, and 120 points. The field
  grows upward while its bottom stays fixed; attach remains at y=481 and Send
  at y=473. The attachment row remains anchored above the composer.
- `04` and `05` show 8 lines still capped at 120 points. Earlier lines are
  clipped above the visible region while lines 3–8 remain visible, confirming
  internal scrolling. The keyboard remains below the whole composer and the
  attachment and controls do not overlap or jump. A disposable E2E-only
  attachment fixture was used after the iOS 26.5 picker returned denial despite
  Settings reporting Full Access; that fixture was reverted before testing.
- `06` shows deletion back to one line and the 40-point baseline. `07` shows
  the empty 40-point composer after Send (Send intentionally dismisses the
  keyboard); the disposable LAN fixture disconnected immediately afterward,
  but the cleared baseline is visible.

Focused verification passed: 2 files and 93 tests. `./kd test all` also passed
with canonical local verification successful.
